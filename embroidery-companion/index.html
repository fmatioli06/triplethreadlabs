Embroidery Companion

A design is puckering, the thread just nested, and the order is due tonight. The Companion is the book you open in that moment: 67 short references covering stabilizers, needles, placement, and the first fix for the problems that stop a job, searchable in seconds.

What it does

Search or browse

Type what you are stitching, or what just went wrong, and go straight to the reference. Seven categories cover the fabric, the setup, the sizing, the fix, digitizing basics, and the business basics around the job.

First checks first

Problem pages lead with the first thing to check, in the order a working shop checks them, so you stop swapping parts at random and find the cause faster.

Starting points that hold up

Needle, stabilizer, and placement numbers are given as honest starting points, with the test stitch as the final word. No fake precision, just the numbers that get you close on the first try.

Bookmarks and quick tools

Bookmark the pages you open every week. Three small tools ride along: a production time estimator, an inch and millimeter converter, and a printable order worksheet.

Why this exists

Every shop has the same moment. A design is puckering, or the thread just nested under the plate, and the answer is somewhere in a forum post, a notebook, or a manual you cannot find while a machine sits idle. The Companion is those answers gathered into one file that opens fast and reads faster.

It is deliberately a lookup book, not a course. You do not read it cover to cover. You search the problem, read the short answer and the first checks, and get back to the machine. The numbers inside are the starting points I use in my own shop, written the way I would want them at 10 pm with an order due: plain, short, and honest about what a test stitch still has to settle.

Two things it is not: it is not a digitizing manual, and it does not price jobs. When a reference touches pricing, it points you to the pricing guide and the calculator, because those questions deserve the full method, not a shortcut.
