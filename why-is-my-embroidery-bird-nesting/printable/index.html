BIRD NESTING FIX ORDER

A nest is loose thread underneath, not a dial problem. Clear it, clean first, rethread, check the bobbin side, and test on scrap. Tension is last.
Clear it. Clean first. Rethread. Bobbin side. Tension last.

READ THE NEST FIRST

  • Nest at the start: setup. Loose tail, thread not seated, bobbin wrong from stitch one.
  • Nest mid-run: something changed. Lint, a dulling needle, thread out of a guide.
  • Front fine, back a mess: top thread looping underneath instead of locking.

CLEAR IT SAFELY

  • Stop as soon as it sounds wrong. Do not yank the hoop free.
  • Cut the wad away in small pieces and free the fabric gently.
  • Get every scrap out. Leftovers start the next nest.
  • Bent or stuck after the thread is out: service, not settings.

CHECK 1: CLEAN FIRST

  • Clean the bobbin area before any setting changes.
  • Lint and tails out from under the plate, per your manual.
  • Bobbin case: check for nicks or a rough edge while it is out.
  • Seat it back exactly the way the manual shows.

CHECK 2: RETHREAD THE TOP

  • Rethread the whole path from the spool, not just the loose end.
  • Thread with the tension discs open so the thread seats. Foot up on most machines.
  • Take-up lever: check it twice. A miss there loops at once.
  • Every guide, in order, the way your manual draws it.

CHECK 3: THE BOBBIN SIDE

  • Bobbin out and back in slowly: seated fully, turning the way your manual shows.
  • Tail not too short, not long and loose. Length per your manual.
  • Winding even, not soft and spongy. Swap a badly wound bobbin.

CHECK 4 AND 5: START, THEN TENSION

  • Fresh needle. A needle that went through a jam is suspect.
  • Hold the thread tail for the first few stitches.
  • Slow the first stitches down. Test on scrap and read the back.
  • Tension last: only if it still loops after checks 1 to 4. Satin column on scrap, bobbin thread down the middle, small steps.
Nest at the start, blame the setup. Nest mid-run, blame what changed. One change, one test.
