Customer Logo & Reorder Hub

Repeat customers are the easiest money in embroidery, but only if you can find their logo, their colors, and what you charged last time. The Hub keeps all of it on one customer record, so the next order starts in seconds instead of a folder hunt.

What it does

Every customer, one profile

Contact details, notes, and every design they have ever ordered, saved once. When they call back, you are looking at their whole history instead of digging through folders and old emails.

Logos and colors, saved

Each design keeps its logo, the embroidery file link, placement, stabilizer, and the exact thread colors you ran. The reorder uses the same colors because they are written down, not remembered.

Order history and reorders

Every past job sits on the customer's profile with what you charged. Pull it up, reorder it in seconds, and the new job starts from the old one's details.

Tracking and backup

A simple order board follows jobs from inquiry to done when you want it. Everything exports to a backup file or CSV, and optional sync shares the library across your computers, still with no subscription.

The problem it solves

A repeat customer is the best order in embroidery: no selling, no guessing, they already trust you. Then you spend twenty minutes hunting for their logo file, trying to remember which blue you used, and pricing the job from memory. The profit in a reorder is speed, and speed dies in the folder hunt.

The Hub kills the hunt. Save the customer once: logo, file, colors, placement, notes, and what they paid. Next time they order, search their name, open the design, and reorder it with everything already filled in. The sample customer loaded in the tool, a football club with one design and two orders, shows exactly how a profile reads.

It is not a pricing tool. You enter your own prices and it keeps the record. Pair it with the pricing system for the numbers and let the Hub remember everything around them.
