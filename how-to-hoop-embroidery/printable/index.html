HOOPING CHECK

Thirty seconds before you press start. The hold, the hoop size, and the alignment decide whether the design stays put, and whether the hoop leaves a mark behind.
Snug, not stretched. Smallest hoop that fits. Layers move as one, or they move apart.

THE SEQUENCE, IN ORDER

  1. Mark the placement first. Center of the design, marked on the garment both ways, before anything goes near a hoop.
  2. Build the sandwich. Backing under the fabric, cut past the hoop edges on every side.
  3. Seat it smooth. Fabric flat and relaxed, wrinkles smoothed outward from the center, never pulled bigger than it rests.
  4. Tighten once seated. Tugging edges afterward is stretch. If it needs a tug, re-seat it.
  5. Nudge the layers. Fabric should not slide on the backing. If it skates, they will move apart mid-run.

ALIGNMENT

  • Straight on the garment, not on the table. Judge by its center line and seams, and how it will be worn.
  • Measure from two fixed points: a seam, a placket, a pocket edge. Same distance on both sides.
  • Grain running true, not twisted in the hoop.
  • Design sitting in the middle of the hoop field, not crowding an edge.

WARNING SIGNS: STOP AND RE-HOOP

  • Ripples in the hooped fabric. It is loose or stretched. Either way it will not settle under the needle.
  • The rings will not close without force. The stack is too thick for this hoop. Float it or clamp it from above.
  • Fabric skates on the backing. Anchor the layers: adhesive, pins outside the design area, or a basting stitch.
  • One loose corner on the tap test. Grip is uneven across the field, and the loose corner is where drift starts.

HOOP BURN

  • Use the least pressure that holds. Every extra crank of force lands on the fabric, not on the stitching.
  • Dark, delicate, or napped fabric: float it, or use a magnetic frame that presses down flat.
  • Marks often relax with steam, a wash, or brushing the nap back up. Test on a scrap of the same fabric first.
  • Hoop when you are ready to stitch, not the night before.
Hold it still, at its natural size, where it belongs. Then press start.
