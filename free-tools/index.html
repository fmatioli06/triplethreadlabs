Thread break check order

Thread breaking mid run? Check these in order before you touch the tension dial. Tension is almost never the first problem, and changing it first just gives you a second one.

1

Needle

Good for 8 to 10 hours of stitching, then it is dull. A dull or burred needle causes most breaks that get blamed on tension. Run a fingernail down the shaft. If it catches, swap it.

2

Thread path

Look for a snag, or a guide it jumped out of.

3

Speed

Drop to about 600 SPM and watch what is actually happening.

4

Tension, last

Half steps, with a test stitch between changes. One change at a time.
