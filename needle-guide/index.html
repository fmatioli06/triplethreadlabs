New to needles? Start here
  • The numbers (like 75/11) are the needle size printed on the pack. Bigger number, thicker needle.
  • Ballpoint means a rounded tip. It is made to slide between the threads, so it suits stretchy fabric.
  • Sharp means a pointed tip. It helps the needle get through tight fabric cleanly.
  • Fit matters when you buy. Home machines usually take needles with one flat side at the top. Commercial machines usually take fully round ones. Your machine manual says which.
  • Thread weight (wt) is how thick the thread is. 40 wt is the standard embroidery thread. Smaller number, thicker thread.
