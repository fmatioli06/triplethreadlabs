The job this prices

Patch sewing is the job shops most often price with a shrug. A rider drops off a vest and a stack of patches, asks what it costs, and the answer is usually a flat number invented on the spot. Then the big jacket back patch takes forty minutes, two patches land on seams, and the shrug turns into a loss.

This calculator prices the work the way it actually happens: patch by patch, by size, at your real hourly rate, with your margin and overhead on top.
